1. Open your phone/laptop
2. Open WiFi sources
3. Connect to *ghostwhisper*
4. Open your web browser and go to "**ghostwhisper.local**"
5. Select your WiFi name and enter credentials
6. Press "Save"
7. If connected the blue led from ESP32 will light up.
